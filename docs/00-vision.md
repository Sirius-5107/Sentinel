# Sentinel Vision

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

---

# Mission

Sentinel is an autonomous financial intelligence platform that continuously monitors global financial markets, filters noise, identifies high-signal developments, and produces institutional-quality investment research.

Its purpose is not simply to summarize news, but to explain why market events matter, connect developments across asset classes and geographies, and build a persistent knowledge base that supports long-term investment research.

---

# Product Vision

Sentinel should function as a personal Bloomberg Terminal for an independent investor.

Every day it should:

- Monitor hundreds of trusted information sources.
- Identify the most important developments.
- Explain their market implications.
- Track recurring themes over time.
- Publish a polished daily briefing.
- Store all knowledge for future retrieval.

Ultimately, Sentinel should evolve into an investment research operating system rather than a news aggregation tool.

# Daily Experience

Reading time:

15 minutes maximum.

The user should finish the daily briefing with a complete understanding of:

- What happened.
- Why it matters.
- Which companies are affected.
- Which sectors are affected.
- What should be monitored next.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Evidence First

Every statement must be backed by reliable sources.

No hallucinations.

No unsupported claims.

---

## 2. Signal over Noise

Twenty articles about one Fed speech should become one insight.

---

## 3. Explain Why

Sentinel always answers:

"So what?"

Not simply:

"What happened?"

---

## 4. Institutional Quality

Writing style should resemble research produced by:

- Goldman Sachs
- Morgan Stanley
- Apollo
- Blackstone
- Bridgewater

---

## 5. Historical Memory

Every report contributes to a growing knowledge base.

Nothing is discarded.

---

## 6. Searchability

Every company.

Every person.

Every event.

Every macro theme.

Every article.

Should be searchable.
